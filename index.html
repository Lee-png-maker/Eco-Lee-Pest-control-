Important Information

Pest Treatment Safety

Pest-control work should always be carried out carefully and according to the requirements of the treatment product being used.

Before treatment

Customers should tell us about children, pets, sensitive areas, food preparation areas and any other relevant property conditions.

  • Follow the treatment instructions provided.
  • Keep people and pets away from treated areas when required.
  • Do not touch or disturb treatment areas unnecessarily.
  • Follow any ventilation or re-entry instructions provided.
  • Ask questions if you are unsure about the treatment.
